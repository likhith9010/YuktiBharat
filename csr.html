Indian Education System : A Start

June 9, 2026

Before we go about fixing Indian Education System especially goverment schools, we should understand hello the problem to begin with , the problems include Inedequate Funding,  Poor Infrastructure, Teacher Shortages, Un / Underqualified Teachers among others . Solving all these issues will take vast resources and time but this blog may give a solution to one of these problems and I personally think it might be the biggest problem why students opting out of goverment schools.

The Problem this blog gives a solution to is Infrastructure.

Solution:

  1. In India we have a law called CSR( Corporate Social Responsibilities ) which requires corporations/companies above 500 cr networth and net profit above 5 cr  to spend 2% of their profit on Social Issues.
  2. In US they have something called SuperPac which is a group of donors , donating money to a cause usually politics. Combining these two ideas we can create a group of companies that comes together to spend their 2% profit for Building of Goverment Schools. I suggest retired Judges, Army Veteran or Teacher to head this group with transperancy.


To also change perception of Government Schools in students. These Schools must be build with world class Infrastructure.Goverment should use it's roster of goverment schools like Kendra Vidyalaya's (KV), Sainik School, Navodaya ... etc cleaverly, for a example Sainik School Should be more incline to Sports comparitvely also Navodaya to be more inclined to Olympiads(Academics). Goverment takes care of Teacher's Salaries, administratient etc .
